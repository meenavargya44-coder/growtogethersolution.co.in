Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Grow Together Solutions handles information submitted through this website.

Information we collect

When you submit the inquiry form we receive the details you enter: name, business email, company name, country, service required, project details and timeline. If you contact us by email or WhatsApp we receive the information you send.

How we use it

We use this information only to respond to your inquiry and discuss or deliver the requested services. We do not sell your information.

Form submissions are delivered to our email through the FormSubmit service, which processes the submission on our behalf.

Files you share for a project are used only for that project. Tell us about any confidentiality requirements before we start.
